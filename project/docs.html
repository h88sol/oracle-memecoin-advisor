ORACLE is an AI advisor for memecoin traders. Ask it anything — about a token, a chart pattern, tokenomics, a rug you can't stop thinking about, the math behind a liquidity pool — by typing or by speaking. It answers in plain language, not financial advice.

What it does

You speak or type a question. The orb listens, thinks, and answers — out loud, on screen, or both. It's a real AI under the hood, tuned to talk fluently about crypto: memecoin mechanics, on-chain signals, trader psychology, and the patterns that separate a launch from a rug.

How to use it

Talking to the orb

Click the microphone button to start a voice query. The orb's halo will turn pink while it's listening. Speak naturally, then pause — it'll send the moment you stop. Or just type into the input and hit Enter.

The orb's states

Ready — calm pulse. Listening — fast pink pulse, ripples speed up. Divining — soft thinking pulse while the AI works. Speaking — tight rings race across the surface as it talks.

Suggested prompts

Below the orb on first load you'll see a few starter questions — tap one to skip the typing.

Tweaks

Open the Tweaks panel from the toolbar to change the orb's color hue, size, particle density, background intensity, the orb's personality, and whether it speaks aloud.

What to ask

"What makes a memecoin pump?"

Liquidity, attention, narrative, and timing. Ask the orb to walk you through any one of those.

"How do I read a token's holder distribution?"

Concentration, dev wallets, fresh wallets, locked LP — the orb explains what to look for and why.

"What's the difference between market cap and FDV?"

A common trap. The orb breaks it down without making you feel dumb for asking.

"Talk me out of aping into this thing."

Sometimes you just need someone to slow you down for ninety seconds.

Important

ORACLE provides perspective and education, not financial advice. Memecoins are extremely high-risk — most go to zero. The orb will not tell you what to buy. Sizing, timing, and risk are yours alone. Do your own research.
