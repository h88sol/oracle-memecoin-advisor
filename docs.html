The peptide advisor that knows you.

PEPTYPE matches peptides to your blood type, current stack, and goal — so you stop guessing and start running protocols built around your actual physiology.

How it works

Three quick questions: your blood type, what peptide you're currently using (if any), and what you want to improve. The orb runs the data through what's known about peptide pharmacology and emerging blood-type-based responses, and gives you a clear verdict on your current protocol plus two recommendations tailored to your profile.

Why blood type matters

Peptide response varies between individuals — and a growing body of research suggests blood type is one factor in how the body processes growth factors, GLP-1s, and healing peptides. O types tend to run higher baseline IGF-1. A types are often more inflammation-sensitive (favoring BPC-157, GHK-Cu). B types often respond well to metabolic peptides like MOTS-c. AB sits between. This is preliminary research, not clinical doctrine — but it's a smarter starting point than treating everyone the same.

What it covers

Recovery (BPC-157, TB-500), GH/sleep (CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, Tesamorelin), body composition (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide), longevity (Epitalon, MOTS-c, GHK-Cu), cognitive (Selank, Semax), and the popular stacks people are actually running.

What it isn't

Not a prescription, not medical advice, not a replacement for blood work or a qualified physician. PEPTYPE is a starting point — a structured way to think through your protocol — not the final word.
