An AI advisor for memecoin traders.

ORACLE is a voice-first AI agent built to help you navigate memecoins. Ask anything — about a token, a rug pattern, tokenomics, liquidity, on-chain signals — and it answers in plain language.

What it is

A real AI under the hood, tuned to talk fluently about crypto. It's not a price feed and it doesn't shill coins. It's a thinking partner — the friend who actually knows what FDV means, who can explain why a chart looks suspicious, and who'll tell you straight when something smells like a rug.

How to use it

Type or speak. Click the mic to talk, or just type a question and hit Enter. The orb listens, thinks, and answers — out loud and on screen.

Three voices. Open the Tweaks panel to switch between Oracle (calm), Analyst (measured), or Degen (chaotic). Same brain, different vibe.

Reset anytime. Hit Reset in the top nav to clear the conversation and start fresh.

FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. ORACLE gives perspective and education only. It will never tell you to buy or sell a specific coin. Sizing, timing, and risk are yours alone.

Can it predict prices?

No, and don't trust anything that claims it can. ORACLE explains mechanics, patterns, and risk — not the future.

How do I check if a token is a rug?

Ask the orb directly. It'll walk you through what to look at — locked liquidity, holder concentration, dev wallets, contract verification, mint authority. Use tools like DexScreener, RugCheck, or GMGN to verify.

What's a memecoin vs a shitcoin?

Loosely: memecoins live on culture and narrative; shitcoins are tokens with no purpose, often launched to dump. The line is blurry — ask the orb to break it down.

Does it know about specific coins?

It knows general crypto and memecoin mechanics. It doesn't pull live prices or chase tickers — for live data check DexScreener, Birdeye, or GMGN.

Is the voice real-time?

Yes — speech-in via your browser, speech-out via the orb. Toggle "Speak aloud" in Tweaks if you want a quiet read instead.

Why is the orb sometimes wrong?

It's an AI. It can be confidently wrong. Treat answers as a starting point, not gospel. Verify anything load-bearing on-chain.

Memecoins are extremely high-risk. Most go to zero. ORACLE will not tell you what to buy. Do your own research.
